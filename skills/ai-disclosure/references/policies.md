# AI policies and player reaction

last_checked: 2026-11-14 (compiled from cited sources — verify on the official pages)

## Steam

- AI disclosure has existed since Jan 2024 (Content Survey).
- Jan 16–17, 2026 update: disclose AI content that players **see or hear** — pre-generated (art, sound, text, marketing assets) and content generated live during play. Valve wrote that "efficiency gains through the use of AI powered tools is not the focus of this section" (e.g. coding assistants).
- An in-overlay button lets players report illegal AI content.
- **Gray area — code:** a games lawyer (Legal Moves Law Firm, Mar 2026) reports Valve confirmed in-game AI-generated content, "code included", is in scope, and classes fully AI-generated code that ships in the game as disclosable while assistive tools (e.g. Copilot) are exempt. → Tell users to read the current form and decide honestly.

| Use | Steam |
|---|---|
| AI coding assistant / autocomplete | Not the focus (exempt per Valve's wording) |
| Fully AI-generated code shipped in the game | Unclear — likely disclose (see lawyer note) |
| AI art / audio / text players see or hear | Disclose |
| AI store page / marketing assets | Disclose |
| Live AI generation in game | Disclose + describe guardrails |

## itch.io

- "Generative AI disclosure" field since 2024-11-20, sub-tags: Graphics, Sound, Text & Dialog, Code.
- Required for assets; from 2026-11-03 also required for soundtracks, books, comics, tabletop/physical games. Untagged AI assets are not indexed in browse.
- From 2026-11-03 the "No AI" filter also applies to search results (visible projects drop from 1.77M+ to < 425K with the filter on).
- Mass-uploaded automated AI content is treated as spam. Traditional algorithms (pathfinding, procedural generation) need no disclosure.

## Reddit

- r/IndieGaming requires disclosure of any GenAI used in the game or its promotion (observed 2026-08).

## Player reaction data

- Game Oracle (Dec 2025), 9,879 paid games Jan–Oct 2025: games with an AI disclosure had a median of 4 reviews in month one vs 7 without; 19.8% vs 15.2% had zero reviews; after controls, ~53% fewer reviews. **Correlation only** — AI-using games may also have smaller budgets and less polish.
- gamedevaihub analysis of 352,296 Steam reviews: where AI use was "discovered", 79% of AI-mentioning reviews were negative; where AI was the selling point, 26%. Art and store images draw the most criticism; code only 2.4% of complaints.
- ~20% of 2025 Steam releases carried an AI disclosure (Totally Human Media / Game Oracle). In 2026 the share rose to 39.1% of releases (Jan 1 – Nov 3) and 49.7% in October 2026 (SteamDB via GamesChart). AI disclosure is now common, so being specific about *what* AI did matters more than whether it was used.

Takeaways: AI-assisted **code** draws little backlash; **visible art**, especially on the store page, draws the most; **concealment** is the worst option.
