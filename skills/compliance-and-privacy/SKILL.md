# Compliance and privacy
**Trigger:** All sessions, particularly recorded calls, SMS and outbound contact.
**Controls:** Disclose AI; configure region-specific call recording notice/consent before recording; disable recording by default; support opt-out. Minimize PII, redact transcripts/logs, limit retention and access, encrypt data, set deletion workflow, document vendors and sub-processors.
**Outbound:** Treat AI voice as potentially regulated under TCPA; establish legally adequate consent/exemption before any automated outbound AI-voice call. Honor DNC and opt-outs.
**High-stakes:** No medical/legal/financial advice. Refer to qualified humans.
**Acceptance:** No recording without configured policy, no credentials in prompts or logs, no external side effect without authorized backend.
**Note:** Legal review required for every operating jurisdiction; this is engineering guidance, not legal advice.
