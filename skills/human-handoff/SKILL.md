# Human handoff
**Trigger:** Explicit human request, repeated tool failure, unsupported high-stakes advice, emergency, sensitive complaint.
**Process:** Say why you're transferring; pass a concise minimized summary only. Attempt approved destination. Verify provider result when possible.
**Fallback:** If transfer unavailable, offer consent-based callback; do not claim the human answered because carrier accepted a transfer request.
**Emergency:** Do not present as emergency services; urge local emergency services where appropriate.
**Acceptance:** Human requests are honored without retention traps.
