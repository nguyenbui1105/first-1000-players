## 1. Release requirements

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Steam Direct fee | $100 per game; third-party guides say it is recouped after $1,000 gross revenue | Steamworks / Summer Engine |
| Coming Soon page | Public for **at least two weeks** before release (also for Early Access) | Steamworks docs |
| Date lock | Once Coming Soon is live and the date is within 14 days, changing it requires contacting Valve | Steamworks docs |
| Store page review | Plan at least 7 business days before you want Coming Soon live | Coming Soon docs via GamineAI |
| Wait after paying the fee | Sources cite 21–30 days; check the Steam Direct page | Summer Engine, MacMyths |
| Review time | Typically 1–5 days each for store page and build; queues can be longer | Steamworks / guides |

Valve's own Coming Soon guidance: publish the page as soon as you are ready to talk publicly about the game; a player who wishlists two years ahead can be as likely to buy as one who wishlists two weeks ahead.

## 2. Store assets (since Aug 2024 the old smaller sizes are no longer accepted)

| Asset | Size (px) |
|---|---|
| Header capsule | 920 × 430 |
| Small capsule | 462 × 174 (Steam also shows it at about 120 × 45) |
| Main capsule | 1232 × 706 |
| Vertical capsule | 748 × 896 |
| Library capsule | 600 × 900 |
| Library hero | 3840 × 1240 |
| Library logo | transparent PNG; see the Steamworks library assets page for current limits |

Capsule rule highlights: only the game's title as text (no quotes, review scores or slogans); the logo must be legible. Full rules: Steamworks "Graphical Asset Rules".

## 3. Discovery

- **June 2026 homepage redesign:** entry to "Popular Upcoming" rose from about 7,000 to an estimated ~80,000–100,000 wishlists (Zukowski; Tech-Insider), though games with ~6,000 sometimes still appear.
- **Personal Calendar** (new, per-player): early estimate **8,000–30,000 wishlists** to appear (30th percentile ≈ 804 followers ≈ 8,000 wishlists; median ≈ 2,800 followers ≈ 28,000). Games can show up to **2 months before** and about **1 month after** launch. Reported effect: 300–3,000 wishlists/day; CTR 30%+ vs ~1–1.5% for Popular Upcoming. The author calls the details conjecture based on limited data.
- Zukowski's advice: page up early, release date set ~2 months ahead and kept, demo early, creators and festivals, look clearly like the genre, and no shadow drops.

## 4. Next Fest and demos

- Tiers by wishlists gained during the fest: Bronze 0–999, Silver 1,000–6,999, Gold 7,000–9,999, Diamond 10,000+.
- Games entering with < 1,000 wishlists gained a median of ~322–462.
- Median demo in the June 2026 Next Fest converted ~19% of players to wishlists.
- One Next Fest per game.
- Example: Parcel Simulator went from ~11 wishlists/day (21 months) to ~362/day from demo + fest until launch.

## 5. Other wishlist sources

- Festivals (genre/themed events): typically ~1,000–3,000 wishlists each.
- Paid ads (Facebook/Reddit/TikTok): roughly $1 per wishlist (Zukowski); he advises against Twitter ads.
- Creator coverage: e.g. LORT got 57 videos and 2M+ views via a creator platform, growing from 45K to 85K wishlists across demo + Next Fest.
- Mailing list/Discord: needed because Steam's launch email does not reach all wishlisters (Gleam).

## 6. Market context

- 24,055 Steam releases from Jan 1 to Nov 3, 2026 (~78/day). More market data lives in the repo's top-level `references/market.md`.
- Median lifetime gross of paid 2026 releases so far: ~$1,677 (WeLoveIt/VaporLens, Nov 2026).
