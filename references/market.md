# Why distribution is the bottleneck — market data

last_checked: 2026-11-14. Steam sales figures are third-party estimates (Valve does not publish sales); methods differ.

## Supply is exploding
- ~20,282 Steam releases in 2025 (~55/day, Chris Zukowski tally); SteamDB counts 19,008–21,188 depending on late additions. 2026 pace ~69.5/day (SteamDB, Aug 2026).
- Only ~608 of 2025 releases (~3%) reached 1,000 reviews; ~49% had 10 reviews or fewer by mid-December.
- Median gross revenue of a 2025 release: ~$249 (Gamalytic, Oct 2025); other methods up to ~$1,700. 65.9% earned under $1,000.

## Money is concentrated, not gone
- 2025 Steam gross ~$16.9B (GameDiscoverCo estimate). 5,863 games passed $100K in 2025 vs ~3,000 in 2020 (Valve, GDC 2026).

## AI-made games are a big, growing share
- Steam games with a GenAI disclosure: ~1,000 (Apr 2024) → 7,818 (Jul 2025) → 10,258 by end of 2025 (~8% of the library, ~20% of 2025 releases). Self-reported; since Jan 2026 coding tools are mostly exempt, so real AI-assisted dev is higher.
- Vibe Jam 2025 received 1,170 games in ~2 weeks; many did not load.
