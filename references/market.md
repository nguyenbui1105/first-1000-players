Notes:
- AI-disclosure counts are a **floor**: they are self-reported, and since January 2026 Steam does not ask about behind-the-scenes tools such as coding assistants. A disclosure only shows that AI was used *somewhere*, including marketing art.
- The median revenue figures above cover different samples (paid games with at least one review vs. all games incl. free-to-play), which is why they differ so much.

## 2025 baseline (full year)

- ~20,282 Steam releases (Chris Zukowski tally); SteamDB counts 19,008–21,344 depending on late additions.
- Only ~608 of 2025 releases (~3%) reached 1,000 reviews; ~49% had 10 reviews or fewer by mid-December.
- Median gross revenue of a 2025 release: ~$249 (Gamalytic, Oct 2025, all games); other methods give up to ~$1,700. 65.9% earned under $1,000.
- ~20–24% of 2025 releases carried an AI disclosure.

## Money is concentrated, not gone

- 2025 Steam gross ~$16.9B (GameDiscoverCo estimate). 5,863 games passed $100K in 2025 vs ~3,000 in 2020 (Valve, GDC 2026).
- H1 2026 Steam gross ~$11.1B, a record half-year (Alinea Analytics, reported by Beacon Digital PR).

## AI-made games

- Vibe Jam 2025 received 1,170 games in ~2 weeks; many did not load.
- Developers with 10+ releases in 2026 disclosed AI on 68% of them, vs 37% for developers with a single game (u/ploz analysis, reported by ShiftDelete).
